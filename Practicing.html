Raphinha

Raphinha is a dynamic Brazilian winger known for his explosive pace, sharp dribbling, and relentless work rate on the right flank. Rising through European football with standout performances at Leeds United, he earned a move to FC Barcelona, where his direct attacking style, high pressing, and ability to deliver decisive crosses and long-range shots quickly made him an important option in the squad. Internationally, Raphinha brings the same intensity and creativity to Brazil, combining flair with tactical discipline—traits that reflect his evolution from a raw wide attacker into a more complete, modern forward capable of influencing matches at both club and national levels.
